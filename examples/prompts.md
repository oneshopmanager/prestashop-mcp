# Example requests

Requests that work with the One Shop Manager MCP server. Changes are filed as proposals: you review and apply them in **AI assistant › AI log**.

## Catalog

- "Which products have a margin below 20%?"
- "List the active products that are out of stock."
- "Which products have no EAN or no cost price?"
- "Which categories are empty?"
- "What sizes already exist for the Size attribute?"

## Bulk changes

- "Lower the price of these products by 15%, rounded to .90, without going under a 25% margin."
- "Disable the out-of-stock products of brand X."
- "Set the stock of these 5 products to 10."
- "Write the missing meta descriptions for the Shoes category, in French and English."
- "Replace 'cotton' with 'organic cotton' in the names of the T-shirts category."

## Products and combinations

- "Create this product from the supplier page: this SKU, this EAN, this price, in the Bags category."
- "Duplicate this T-shirt as a kids' version."
- "Create a Shoe size attribute with sizes 36 to 46."
- "Add the S, M, L and XL combinations to this sweatshirt."
- "Fill in the EANs of these combinations."

## Orders

- "Which paid orders have not shipped yet?"
- "Here are today's tracking numbers: … Set the orders to Shipped and email the customers."

## Analysis

- "Sales summary for September: revenue, margin, top customers."
- "What has not sold in the last 90 days, by stock value tied up?"
- "What should I reorder to cover 30 days of sales?"
- "How much is my stock worth?"
- "What are the blocking SEO issues?"
- "Create a table of revenue by country and by month."

## Accounting

- "VAT collected by country in the third quarter?"
- "Which October invoices are unbalanced?"
- "Why is order XKBKNABJK unbalanced?"

## Journal

- "What was changed today?"
- "Undo yesterday's price drop."
